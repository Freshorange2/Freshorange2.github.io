Research field

Spatial cognition

This field investigates how people perceive and attend to spatial information, encode and integrate it into cognitive maps, and retrieve and update those representations to support spatial reasoning, decision-making, and navigation. It examines these processes across real-world and simulated environments, from everyday urban settings to extreme and extraterrestrial contexts. It uses a multimodal cognitive neuroscience approach that combines behavioral measures, eye tracking and pupillometry, electrophysiology (EEG), functional neuroimaging (fMRI), and peripheral physiological measures to reveal the mechanisms underlying human spatial cognition.
