Research field

Human-centered GeoAI

This field translates empirical knowledge of human perception, attention, spatial learning, decision-making, and navigation into computational principles for GeoAI. It develops brain-inspired models of spatial coding, cognitive-map formation, landmark anchoring, and route planning, alongside cognition-informed systems that recognize users’ spatial tasks and cognitive states from behavioral and multimodal neurophysiological data. The aim is to create geospatial AI that can interpret complex geographic environments, reason and navigate under uncertainty, and adapt to human abilities, intentions, and experience.
