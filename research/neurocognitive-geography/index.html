Research field

Neurocognitive geography

This field integrates geography and cognitive neuroscience to investigate the bidirectional relationships among geographic environments, the human brain, and behavior. It examines how urban, natural, cultural, and disaster-affected environments shape cognition, emotion, brain development, and everyday behavior, and how individuals and communities, through their decisions and spatial practices, in turn adapt to and reshape those environments. Across spatial scales and the human lifespan, it integrates neural, behavioral, and physiological evidence with geospatial data and spatial analysis to address questions of sustainable cities, health and well-being, climate adaptation, and regional inequalities.
