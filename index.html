Research profile

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Geography at Ghent University. I hold a PhD in Geomatics and Surveying from Ghent University. My research connects GIScience, spatial cognition, neurocognitive geography, and human-centered GeoAI.

My work examines the cognitive mechanisms that shape human perception, attention, and navigation in geographic and urban environments, and translates empirical evidence about these mechanisms into cognition-informed computational models.

My current research agenda advances urban cognitive geography by scaling behavioral and neurophysiological evidence from individual-level studies to urban-scale analysis, while developing GeoAI methods that better align with human spatial cognition and experience.
